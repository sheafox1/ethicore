How It Works

Planning, construction, closeout

How an Ethicore project runs, in three phases.

1. Consultation

Talk through what you want to change, how you use the space, and your budget and timing.

2. Planning

We measure, draw plans to scale and price every selection before demolition.

3. Construction

Your project manager runs demolition, rough-ins, inspections and finish work on one schedule.

4. Closeout

A punch list, final inspections and your records handed over.
